# Wall of Shame

The **Wall of shame** is open to everyone, signed in or not. It lists accounts that administrators have banned, with the message the administrator entered when banning.

- Each entry shows the account's name and the ban message.
- An entry with an end date shows **Until** and that date.
- Long lists are paged.
- When nobody is banned the page says so.

A banned account cannot sign in and is signed out of all its sessions. An administrator can lift a ban at any time; see [Managing users](./managing-users.md#banning-and-unbanning).
